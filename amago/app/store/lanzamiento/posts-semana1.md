## Día 1 · r/WebGames

**Título:** AMAGO – a 1v1 blind duel: you can't see your opponent, only their footprints (plays in the browser, no install)

**Texto:**
I made a small 1v1 game where both players are hidden on their own island. You only see the footprints your rival leaves when they move, so every shot is a guess, and you can leave fake footprints to trick them.

- Rounds last about 2 minutes
- 18 heroes with different attacks (arrows, mines, lightning…)
- Online or against the computer, works on phone and desktop

It's free and has no ads. I'd love to hear what feels unfair or confusing, I'm fixing things every day.
