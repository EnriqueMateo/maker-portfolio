AMAGO Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Summary: You can play as a guest without giving any data. If you want, you can create an account with your email and a password to keep your progress and use it on another phone. AMAGO shows no ads and uses no third-party tracking tools. Your progress is stored on your device, with a copy on our server linked to an anonymous ID so you don't lose it. We also keep anonymous usage statistics to improve the game.

Who is responsible

AMAGO is a game developed by [NOMBRE DEL RESPONSABLE]. For any privacy question you can write to [CORREO DE CONTACTO].

What data is processed and why

  • Your progress (trophies, coins, heroes, settings and your player name): stored on your device, with a copy on our server linked to an anonymous ID created by the app (it is not your name, email or a device identifier). It lets you keep your progress and move it to another phone with a code. It is kept while you play and deleted after 24 months without use.
  • Your account (only if you create one): your email and your password, which we store hashed (never in plain text). They are only used so you can sign in from any phone; we don't send you advertising or share them. They are kept while you have the account and deleted with Settings → Delete account and progress.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: which screens you open, when you start and finish matches, how long rounds last and which heroes you use, linked to the same anonymous ID. They help us know whether the game is understood and fun (for example, how many people come back the next day). Kept for at most 24 months.
  • Online match trophies: calculated and stored by the server so matchmaking is fair.
  • When you play online your player name, trophies and heroes are sent to the server to match you with a rival of similar level and to run the match. During the match they are only used to play it.
  • What your rival sees: in Online mode your rival sees an automatic nickname, not the name you type. In private rooms with friends, your friends see your player name.
  • IP address and technical connection data: processed by the server and its hosting provider (Railway) to make the connection work and to protect the game from abuse (for example, limiting connections per IP). We don't use them for anything else.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads or use ad networks.
  • We don't use third-party analytics tools or advertising identifiers.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We don't ask for your phone number, location, photos or contacts. Your email is only requested if you choose to create an account.

Purchases

The app currently has no real-money purchases. If they are enabled in the future, payments will be handled by Apple through the App Store or by Google Play; we never receive your card details.

Children

AMAGO doesn't ask for personal data to play. If you are under 14 (or the age of digital consent in your country), play online with your parent's or guardian's permission. Parents can use iOS Screen Time or Google Family Link to limit online play or purchases.

Your rights

To delete your data, use Settings → Delete progress (or Delete account and progress if you have an account) in the game: it is removed from your device and our server. You can also exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection and portability by writing to the contact email. If you believe we haven't handled your data properly, you can file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
